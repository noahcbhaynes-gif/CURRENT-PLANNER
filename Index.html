Privacy Policy for Current

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Current is a desktop scheduling and productivity application for Windows.

Data collection

Current does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking services.

Local data

Information entered into Current, including activities, schedules, available time, goals, and application preferences, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide the functionality of the application and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Internet connectivity

Current does not require an internet connection for its core functionality and does not transmit users' schedule information or other app data to external servers.

Downloads and updates provided through the Microsoft Store may involve Microsoft services and are governed by Microsoft's own privacy practices.

Third parties

Current does not provide user data to advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

Changes to this policy

If Current's data practices change in the future, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
