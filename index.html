Privacy Policy for Current

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Current is a desktop scheduling and productivity application for Windows.

Data handling

Current does not transmit, sell, or share personal information. Data you enter into the app is stored only on your device.

Local data

Activities, schedules, available time, goals, preferences, and custom sounds are stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide the functionality of the application and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Analytics and tracking

Current does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or remote crash reporting.

Internet connectivity

Current does not require an internet connection for its app functionality and does not transmit schedule information, preferences, or other app data to external servers.

Downloads and updates distributed through the Microsoft Store may use Microsoft services and are governed by Microsoft's own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If Current's data practices change in the future, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
